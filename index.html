Log your hours — quick guide

Open the app

📱 Open on your phone 💻 Open on a computer

Crew and foremen use the phone version. Office/PMs use the computer version.

First time: sign in & save to your phone

  1. Tap Open on your phone above.
  2. Sign in with the email and password the office gave you.
  3. Save it to your home screen so it opens like an app:

    iPhone (Safari)

    Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.

    Android (Chrome)

    Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → Add to Home screen → Add.
  4. From now on, just tap the icon on your home screen.
You'll only see your own hours. If you sign in and see nothing or can't enter time, your login probably isn't linked yet — let the office know.

Trade Logging your hours

  1. Open the app — it lands on your week.
  2. Use ‹ Prev / Next › to get to the right week.
  3. For each day you worked, tap + add line.
  4. Choose the task you worked on (or ⚠ Wastage for non-productive time like rain delays).
  5. Enter your hours for that line. Add a short note if useful.
  6. Add more lines if you split the day across tasks. That's it — it saves as you go.
Every line must be a task or Wastage. Once a foreman approves a line it locks (shows ✓ Approved) and you can't change it — see the office if something needs fixing.

Foreman Reviewing & approving

  • Switch the person picker to 👥 All people to see everyone's hours for the week, grouped by person.
  • Tap ✎ on someone's line (or open their week) to check it.
  • Tap Approve on each correct line — it then locks for the crew member.
  • To fix something, tap ✓ Approved → unlock, edit, and re-approve.
You can't approve your own hours, or any line you entered or last edited — someone else signs those off.

PM / Admin Office

  • Use the computer version for the full picture: the weekly grid, summary, and costs.
  • People tab — add crew, set their hourly rate, and set each person's Level (Trade / Foreman / PM). You can only manage people below your own level.
  • Approve hours in the week pop-up, the same way foremen do.
  • Hit Reload to pull the latest after others have been editing.
Trouble signing in or something looks wrong?
  • I see nothing / can't add hours — your login isn't linked to you yet. Contact the office.
  • Forgot my password — the office can reset it.
  • Someone else's edits aren't showing — tap Reload.
  • A line is greyed out — it's been approved and is locked. The office can reopen it.
